# Issue Tracker

This document is used to track and manage issues encountered during the development of the LPR-SingleDrone project.

---

## How to use this template:

When a new issue is identified, copy and paste the template below and fill in the details.

---

## Issue Template

### Issue ID: [Unique ID, e.g., LPR-001]
- **Status:** [Critical | High | Medium | Low | Resolved | Deferred]
- **Title:** [Concise summary of the issue]
- **Description:**
  A detailed explanation of the problem, including:
  - What is happening.
  - Expected behavior vs. actual behavior.
  - Steps to reproduce (if applicable).
  - Any error messages or logs.
- **Affected Files/Components:**
  - List of files, modules, or services that are impacted.
  - Example: `gpu/lpr/producer.py`, `central/server/api.ts`
- **Previous Attempts/Notes:**
  - Summarize any attempts made to resolve the issue.
  - Key observations or theories.
  - Why previous attempts failed (if applicable).
