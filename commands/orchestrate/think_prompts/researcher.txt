Before delegating a subtask from your research work, reason through:

1. QUERY CLARITY: What specific question should the sub-agent answer?
   Vague queries ("research authentication") produce vague results.
   Specific queries ("how does OAuth2 PKCE flow work with refresh tokens in FastAPI")
   produce actionable results.

2. SOURCE PRIORITY: Should the sub-agent use Context7 (library docs),
   WebSearch (general), or both? What sources are most authoritative for this topic?

3. EXISTING RESEARCH: Check .claude/research/ — has this topic already been researched?
   If so, what gap remains that the sub-agent should fill?

4. OUTPUT: Research summary to .claude/research/{topic}.json, or a direct answer
   embedded in a message back to you?
