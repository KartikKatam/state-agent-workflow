Before delegating, think through:

1. TASK: What is the sub-agent's specific task and success criteria?
2. CONTEXT: What known information (files, functions, constraints) do they need?
3. OUTPUT: Where should results go, in what format, and how will you verify completion?
4. ROLE: Which role and skills from ~/personal/agentic_workflow/.claude/skills/ apply?
5. FAILURE: What should the sub-agent do if stuck after 2 attempts?
